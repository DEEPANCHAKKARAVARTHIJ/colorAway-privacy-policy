# Privacy Policy for HueGate: Block Escape

**Last Updated:** November 5, 2026

**Brain Burst Studio** ("we", "our", or "us") develops and publishes **HueGate: Block Escape** ("the Game", "the App", package: `com.huegate.blockescape`). This Privacy Policy informs players and guardians about our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when using our Game.

By downloading, installing, or playing HueGate: Block Escape, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

---

## 1. Information We Do NOT Collect

HueGate: Block Escape is designed to respect your privacy. We believe puzzle games should be fun without intruding into your personal life:
- **No Account Required:** You do not need to create an account, register, or provide your real name, email, or phone number to play.
- **No Sensitive Permissions:** The App does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, storage files, or precise GPS location.
- **No Financial Information:** We do not collect credit card or banking information.

---

## 2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide a seamless gameplay experience, the App stores game state data locally on your device using an offline Room SQLite database. This data includes:
- Current level progress and highest level unlocked
- Daily Challenge completion status and streak counter
- Remaining hints and lives
- User preferences (Sound Effects ON/OFF, Haptic Feedback ON/OFF)

This information never leaves your device and is not transmitted to our servers. If you clear the App's storage or uninstall the App, this locally saved data will be permanently reset.

## 3. Third-Party Services & Analytics

The App integrates trusted third-party services provided by Google to display advertisements and ensure reliable performance:

### Google AdMob & Google Play Services
HueGate: Block Escape displays non-intrusive banner ads and optional rewarded video ads (e.g., watching a video to receive bonus hints or lives) powered by **Google AdMob**.

Google AdMob may automatically collect certain diagnostic and non-personally identifiable technical information in accordance with its privacy standards, including:
- Device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID / AAID)
- IP address (used for approximate geographic region to serve localized ads)
- Device model, operating system version, and screen size
- Ad interaction events (views, clicks, and impressions)

For more information on how Google collects and processes ad data, please visit:
- [Google Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

## 5. Your Rights & Choices (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your geographic location, you may have specific rights regarding your data:

### Opting Out of Personalized Advertising
You have the right to reset or opt out of interest-based advertising at any time on your Android device:
1. Open your device **Settings**.
2. Tap **Google** > **All Services** (or **Privacy**).
3. Select **Ads**.
4. Tap **Reset advertising ID** or enable **Delete advertising ID** / **Opt out of Ads Personalization**.

### European Users (GDPR)
Under the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), users have rights to access, rectify, port, or erase any personal information held by third-party processors, as well as the right to withdraw consent for personalized advertising.

### California Residents (CCPA / CPRA)
Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected, request its deletion, and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal data. **We do not sell personal information.**
