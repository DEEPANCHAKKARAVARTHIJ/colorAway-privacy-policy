Privacy Policy

HueGate: Block Escape (com.huegate.blockescape)
Last Updated: November 5, 2026

Brain Burst Studio ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our mobile application, HueGate: Block Escape ("the Game", "the App").

By downloading and playing HueGate: Block Escape, you agree to the terms described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

Key Privacy Guarantee: HueGate: Block Escape does not require any account creation, login, or personal profile. We respect your anonymity.
  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not collect your real name, email address, postal address, or telephone number.
  • No Sensitive Permissions: We do not access your device's camera, microphone, photo gallery, address book, or precise GPS location.
  • No Financial Data: We do not collect payment or banking information.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

To ensure your puzzle progress is never lost, the Game saves non-personal game progress locally on your device using a private SQLite database. This includes:

  • Current level reached and completed puzzle records
  • Daily Challenge completion history and streak count
  • Remaining hints and lives
  • Audio and haptics preferences (Sound Effects ON/OFF, Vibration ON/OFF)

This data remains on your physical device and is never uploaded to our servers.

3. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep the Game free to play, HueGate: Block Escape displays advertisements powered by Google AdMob (Google LLC), including bottom banner ads and optional rewarded video ads.

Google AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous technical data in compliance with Google's Privacy Policy, such as:

  • Google Advertising Identifier (AAID)
  • Coarse / approximate geographic location (country or city level derived from IP address)
  • Device model, operating system version, and system language
  • Ad engagement metrics (impressions, clicks, and video completion events)

Learn more about how Google handles ad data: Google Privacy Policy and How Google Uses Information From Sites or Apps.

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

HueGate: Block Escape is a general-audience casual puzzle game designed for players of all ages. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international child privacy laws:

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA).
  • Advertisements are configured to adhere to Google Play Families Policy guidelines.
  • If a parent or guardian discovers that personal data was inadvertently transmitted, please contact us and we will promptly remove it.

5. Your Choices & Opt-Out Options

You can control or opt out of personalized advertisements directly through your Android device settings:

  • Go to Settings > Google > Ads.
  • Tap Delete advertising ID or Reset advertising ID.

6. Security

We value your trust. All ad network communications use secure TLS/HTTPS encryption, and local game data is kept inside the app's sandboxed storage.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated "Last Updated" timestamp.
